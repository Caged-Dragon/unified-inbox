Privacy policy

Unified Inbox, by Caged Dragon Studios. Last updated October 2, 2026. Back to the app home page.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with a Google account, the app asks for permission to use your Gmail. It uses that permission to read your mail, labels, drafts and conversations, to send mail you write, to change labels and folders, and to read and change your signature, auto-reply and filters. It also reads the sender and recipient addresses in your mail to suggest contacts when you write a message.

Where your data goes

Your mail is requested directly from Google and shown on your phone when you use the app. Unified Inbox has no server. The developer does not receive, store, view or sell your mail or any other Google user data.

What is stored on your phone

  • Sign-in tokens for each account you add, kept in the app's private storage on your device.
  • A list of email addresses seen in your mail, used only for contact suggestions.
  • A short list of message identifiers, used only to decide which new mail to alert you about.
  • Attachments, only when you tap Save. They go to your Downloads folder.

Uninstalling the app deletes the data it stores.

Sharing

The app does not share your data with anyone. It has no advertising and no analytics.

Background checks

About every 15 minutes the app asks Google whether you have new unread mail, so it can show a notification. You can turn this off in Settings inside the app.

Your choices

You can remove an account from the app at any time. You can also withdraw the app's access in your Google Account under Security, in the list of third-party apps with access to your account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Unified Inbox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
