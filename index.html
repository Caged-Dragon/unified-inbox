Unified Inbox

Unified Inbox is an Android app that brings several Gmail accounts into one inbox on your own phone. It is a personal project by Caged Dragon Studios.

What the app does

  • Sign in with any number of Gmail accounts and read them together or one at a time.
  • Read, search, star, archive, delete, label and report spam on conversations.
  • Write, reply to and forward mail, choosing which account sends it. Drafts and attachments are supported.
  • Copy or move conversations between your own accounts.
  • Manage each account's signature, auto-reply and filters, and get alerts for new mail.

Your data

The app talks directly to Google. There is no Unified Inbox server, and the developer never receives your mail. Read the privacy policy for the full details.
